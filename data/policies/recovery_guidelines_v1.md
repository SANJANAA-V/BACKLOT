# Product Recovery Guidelines (SAMPLE, fictional)
Version: 1.0

## RG-1 Human review gate
Any case with incomplete critical evidence, a high-severity finding, a flagged risk score or photograph differences goes to a human reviewer before a recovery action is taken. Automated scores and model-generated text never finalise an irreversible decision.

## RG-2 Resale
Grade A and Grade B products that pass inspection and are not restricted by policy are returned to resale stock.

## RG-3 Refurbish and repair
Used products are refurbished when the expected resale value exceeds the refurbishment cost by the minimum margin. Damaged products are repaired only when repair is clearly more valuable than liquidation.

## RG-4 Liquidation and recycling
Products that are not worth repairing are liquidated when liquidation recovers more than its handling cost, otherwise they are recycled responsibly.
