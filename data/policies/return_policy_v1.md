# Return Policy (SAMPLE, fictional)
Version: 1.0

## RP-1 Eligibility and condition reporting
Customers report the condition of the item when requesting a return. The reported condition is recorded as the customer's account, not as a verified fact, and is compared against warehouse inspection.

## RP-2 Evidence inconsistencies are not accusations
A mismatch between customer-reported condition and warehouse inspection, or between recorded and expected parcel weight, is an evidence inconsistency. It must be reviewed by a person before any refund is withheld. Possible benign causes must be considered: transit damage, scale error, accessories included, grading differences.

## RP-3 Wrong or mismatched item
If the product identifier observed at the warehouse differs from the identifier shipped, the case must be escalated to human review. The reviewer re-verifies the identifier, checks for scanning or data-entry errors, and contacts the customer before any decision.

## RP-4 Empty or missing item in package
If the warehouse reports the item missing from the package, or the parcel weight is far below the expected weight, the case must be escalated to human review. Check carrier custody records and whether the item was lost in transit before concluding anything.

## RP-5 Damage and responsibility
Damage found at the warehouse may have occurred before pickup or during carrier transit. Damage the customer disclosed at the time of the request is treated differently from undisclosed damage. When the origin cannot be determined from the evidence, the case is recorded as uncertain.

## RP-6 Hygiene-restricted products
Opened hygiene-restricted products, such as headphones and footwear, must not be resold as new. They may be refurbished and sanitised, or liquidated.
