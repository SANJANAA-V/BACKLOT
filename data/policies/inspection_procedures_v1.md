# Warehouse Inspection Procedures (SAMPLE, fictional)
Version: 1.0

## WI-1 Condition grading
Grade A: unopened. Grade B: opened, fully working, cosmetically clean. Grade C: used with visible wear. Grade D: damaged or used with damage. Inspectors record the observed condition and upload photographs of the product and packaging.

## WI-2 Parcel weight check
Parcel weight is compared with the expected product and packaging weight. A deviation up to 5 percent is normal. Larger deviations are recorded as a weight discrepancy. Accessories or extra packaging can explain smaller overages; a much lighter parcel suggests a missing item.

## WI-3 Product identifier verification
Inspectors scan the serial number or product identifier and compare it with the identifier of the shipped unit. A mismatch must be re-scanned once before it is recorded, because scanner and typing errors are common.

## WI-4 Photographic evidence
Photographs from both the customer and the warehouse should be attached. Differences between the two, such as damage visible in only one, are noted for the reviewer. Photograph analysis is advisory and does not verify identity or authenticity.

## WI-5 Chain of custody and missing scans
Every return should have pickup scheduled, pickup completed, hub received and warehouse inspected events. A missing logistics scan creates a custody gap. The case should be reviewed because damage or loss cannot be attributed with confidence.

## WI-6 Duplicate and delayed events
Duplicate events from the logistics system are common and are deduplicated. Delays over 72 hours between logistics steps are noted but are usually carrier related.
